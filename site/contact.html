Contact

One inbox, read by the person who maintains the site. No ticket system, no chat widget, no phone number — those would be decoration on a site this size.

Email is the only channel
Write to
contact@animefightingsimulators.com

Worth emailing about

  • A code that came back dead. Tell us which one and what the game said. Rejected codes get pulled or moved to the disputed block, and your report is what moves them.
  • A number that looks wrong. Reward amounts, boost counts, currencies. Screenshots help but are not required.
  • Something missing or unclear. If a page left you with a question it should have answered, that is a defect worth reporting.
  • Privacy requests. Asking what we hold, or asking us to delete mail you have sent. Both are handled, see the privacy policy.
  • Advertising questions. Including which vendor served an ad to you.

What we cannot help with

Expired codes

Once a code has been rotated out by the developers, no site can bring it back. We can only take it off the list faster.

Account, ban, or purchase problems

Those sit with Roblox or with the game's developers. We have no access to either and cannot look into an account.

Lost progress or missing items

Same reason. Rewards that did not arrive after a successful redemption are a developer-side issue.

Code requests

We publish codes the developers release. We do not have a supply of unreleased ones to hand out.

On reply times. There is no promise to make here. Mail gets read, corrections get made, but this is a small site and a fast answer is not something to commit to. Time-sensitive matters are better raised in the game's own community channels.
