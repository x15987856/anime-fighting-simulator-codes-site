Anime Fighting Simulator Map

There is no single picture to open. The world is one overworld of training areas, with dimensions sitting behind portals on top of it, and every spawn drops you into Dimension 1. Below: how to tell where you are, and the parts no source has written down.

Checked 15 September 2026 · Current build: Update 9 (Skill Tree)

The short answer

Where the map is

One overworld carries the training areas. Dimensions are separate spaces you enter through portals. You load into Dimension 1.

How you view it

No page documents a map or minimap screen in this build. You find your way by landmark and by the training-area ladder.

Which version this is

Update 9 — the build that shipped the code UPDATE9. The fan wiki's own update log still stops at Update 5, so area lists elsewhere lag.

What is missing everywhere

Nothing we could read maps areas to dimensions, publishes coordinates, or says how many dimensions the current build has.

Four different games answer to this name

Search for this keyword and you get pages about at least four separate Roblox experiences mixed together. Their maps are not the same map. Sorting out which one you are playing is most of the battle.

Why this trips people up

A minimap does exist somewhere in this franchise — just not in the game you are probably playing. It belongs to the title called X. If you followed a guide that told you to click an ellipsis icon and pick a map icon, that instruction is for a different game.

The training-area ladder is how you locate yourself

With no map screen to open, the practical way to read the world is the ladder. Each stat has its own chain of areas, and each area has a power requirement you must meet before it will train you. Whichever rung you currently qualify for tells you roughly where in the world you belong — and it is the same information the game gates your progress on.

That is 68 documented areas across five stats. Speed and Agility stop early — three and two rungs — so once you are past 100K in either, there is nowhere further to stand and the other three stats become your only progression path.

The gate, and the one number that looks wrong. An area will not train you until the matching stat meets its requirement, so standing on a higher rung and getting nothing means you are short, not that the area is broken. Two multipliers on the wiki look mistyped: Motorless Treadmill and Gravity Chamber are both listed as x15K while every comparable rung on the other ladders reads x15. We left the rest as published and are flagging these two rather than quietly correcting them.

Dimensions

Dimensions are the second layer of the world. Each one carries exactly one Dimension Boss, and that boss is the cleanest way to identify which dimension you have walked into.

  • Dimension 1 — Kurama. This is where you spawn. Kurama drops 50 Chikara at Tier 1, rising by 20 per tier to Tier 5, and its base HP at Tier 1 is 1.25K.
  • Dimension 2 — Broly. Three phases, each one healing him to full when the previous ends. He drops 75 Tensei Shards at Tier 1, rising by 30 per tier.

Boss fights run on a two-minute clock. Miss it and everyone in the arena is teleported back to spawn with nothing, no matter how much damage landed. Bosses carry a Tier from 1 to 5 that climbs on each win and drops on each loss, and tiers are shared across the whole server rather than tracked per player.

Honest gap — dimension count

No page we could read states how many dimensions the current build has. The 2019 original's wiki describes portals to "the other 5 dimensions," which implies six — but that page is about the older game, and we are not treating it as an answer for this one. How many exist in Update 9 is unknown.

What nobody has written down

No map screen, documented

No page we could read documents a map, world map, or minimap screen for Update 9. That is not the same as proof that none exists — it means nobody has written one down.

Areas are not mapped to dimensions

The current wiki lists all 68 areas as one flat ladder per stat. Which dimension each area physically sits in is unwritten.

No coordinates

Nothing publishes grid references, coordinate pairs, or measured distances. Every location description on every page is prose and landmarks only.

Portals, in the current build

Portal locations are documented for the 2019 original — above the lake, across from Monster. For Update 9, no page we could read says where the portals stand.
