Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to your information when you read Anime Fightings Simulators. Short version: the site carries third-party advertising, that advertising uses cookies, and nothing here asks you to hand over personal details.

Last updated 14 September 2026

Advertising and cookies

This site is free to read because it carries advertising from third-party ad vendors. Those vendors set and read cookies and similar technologies in your browser. They use them to select which ad you see, to stop showing you the same ad repeatedly, and to count how many people saw or clicked it.

That means a third party — not us — receives information about your visit, including your IP address, the page you loaded, your browser type, and your device. We do not control what those vendors do with it, and their handling of it is governed by their own policies, not this one.

Advertising vendors rotate. Rather than print a list that goes stale, we describe the practice here. If you want to know which vendor is serving on the site today, email us and we will tell you.

How to turn the cookies off

Every major browser can block or delete cookies, and most now ship with a setting that rejects third-party cookies specifically. Turning that on will stop ad tracking across the web, including here. The trade-off: ads still appear, they just stop being matched to you. Clearing cookies in a browser that has already accepted them removes the ones already stored.

What we collect ourselves

Very little, because there is no mechanism to collect more. This site has no login, no account system, no comment field, no newsletter signup, and no checkout. You are not asked for your name, your address, a phone number, or payment details anywhere on it.

  • If you email us — we have your email address and whatever you chose to write. That is the only personal information that reaches us directly.
  • If you only read the site — the sole information path is the advertising cookies described above.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or hand over email we receive. Mail sent to us is used to answer the question it contains, and to fix errors on the site when the mail points one out.

Children

The game this site covers is played by a lot of young people, so this matters here. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a child has emailed us and a parent or guardian wants that message and address deleted, write to us and we will remove it.

Links leaving this site

As things stand, these pages link only to each other. There are no outbound links to third-party websites anywhere on this site.

That is a description of today, not a promise about forever. The rule that will apply if outbound links are added is the ordinary one: the moment you follow a link off this site, you are on someone else's site under someone else's policy, and what that site does with your information is not something we control or answer for.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes — a new category of data, a new kind of tracking — get called out explicitly rather than slipped in.

Getting in touch

Questions about any of the above, or a request to delete mail you have sent us, go to contact@animefightingsimulators.com. You can also use the contact page.

Anime Fightings Simulators is an independent site. It is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or with the developers of Anime Fighting Simulator.
